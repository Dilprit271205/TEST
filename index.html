Reachability starts with the company graph. MITRE ATT&CK describes plausible behaviour, STRIDE frames threats, and a CVE contributes only when a matching component is on the route. 07 / rank the branches

Which path
matters most?

Compare each route's illustrative 24-hour probability, potential harm and synthetic monetary exposure. A leads at first because reporting activity and a database attempt were observed. Inject a new export request, then try a hypothetical independently verified change ticket. The raw request stays recorded while its added suspicion is reconsidered.

Context design rule: an independent reviewer verifies the ticket; the requester cannot approve it. The original alert is retained. No approval exists in the base E-104 case.

Route risk index = probability × harm / 100. Route-level money = probability × synthetic loss if that route succeeds. Shared assets are counted once in the portfolio replay, not by adding these route figures.

09 / defensive counterfactual

Find the cut.
Prove it on the twin.

The highest-risk route is selected automatically from the current evidence. The system traces its graph edges, tests possible breakpoints in a copied case graph, and replays 4,096 synthetic trials for each allowed intervention.

Calculating the lowest-cost cut across the modeled routes.

DEMO OBJECTIVE · J = residual expected damage + 0.15 × mean damage in the worst 5% of trials + action cost (₹ lakh ÷ 100) + 0.01 × disruption points (₹ crore). Constraints: action budget ₹1.20 lakh; critical service available; reversible controls; selected route cut; 95% upper bound on protected-target reach ≤5%. Among equally protective choices, lower cost and disruption win.

The recommended cut appears automatically.

Synthetic assumptions: A ₹5 crore, B ₹7 crore to the shared customer DB, C ₹8 crore to billing storage. Conditional route outcomes are sampled independently; A and B hitting the same asset count once at the larger loss. These are projected losses, not a real breach estimate. Bayesian calibration and production controls are planned; verify independent tokens before acting.
