06 / path assessment

Evidence changes
our belief.

Reachability comes from the company graph. MITRE ATT&CK describes possible adversary behaviour; STRIDE frames threats and consequences; a CVE matters only if a matching vulnerable component sits on a route. None of these labels alone is a probability.

Intended Bayesian MCMC: estimate the chance of a defined route reaching its outcome in the next 24 hours, with uncertainty. Potential harm depends on asset sensitivity, access scope and business effect.
